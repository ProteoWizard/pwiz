CarafeSharp command-line usage

CarafeSharp - spectral library prediction and fine-tuning for Osprey (C# port of Carafe)

USAGE: CarafeSharp --build_entrapment_fasta <out.fasta> --db <proteins.fasta> [options]

CarafeSharp --reconcile_manifest <out.tsv> --manifest <in.tsv> --predicted_library <library.tsv|.blib>

CarafeSharp --db <peptides.fasta|proteins.fasta> -o <folder> [options] (library prediction)

CarafeSharp -i <osprey.blib> --ms <runs> -o <folder> [--db <fasta>] [options] (training)

CarafeSharp -i <run.training.parquet|folder> [--ms <runs>] -o <folder> [--db <fasta>] [options]

CarafeSharp --model_info <file.carafemodel>

Modes and files
ArgumentDescription
--build_entrapment_fasta <out.fasta>Build the peptide FASTA a library is predicted from: the digest of the proteins of --db, with decoys and, with --entrapment, entrapment peptides; the pairing manifest goes to --manifest.
--reconcile_manifest <out.tsv>Write the pairing manifest of --manifest reconciled against the predicted library of --predicted_library, dropping the pairs the library did not keep whole.
--manifest <manifest.tsv>The pairing manifest: written by --build_entrapment_fasta, read by --reconcile_manifest.
--predicted_library <library.tsv|.blib>The predicted library (a TSV, or a .blib) a manifest is reconciled against.
--model_info <file.carafemodel>Describe a saved model (.carafemodel): what it was trained on, and how.
--db <proteins.fasta|peptides.fasta>The FASTA to predict a library from (peptides, as --build_entrapment_fasta writes them, or proteins), or the proteins --build_entrapment_fasta digests. With training (--i), the library predicted with the fine-tuned models afterwards.
--i <osprey.blib|run.training.parquet|folder>Osprey's results to train on: its .blib with --ms, or its training exports (one run's .training.parquet, a comma-separated list of them, or a folder of them) with or without --ms. With a .blib and no --ms a library is predicted instead, as in Carafe.
--ms <run1,run2,...>The runs trained on, comma-separated. CarafeSharp reads no spectra: the fragment matches, XICs and peak boundaries come from Osprey's training export, so --ms only names the runs.
--o <folder>The output folder: the library, and for training the fine-tuned model folder and the saved .carafemodel.
-h, --help [<format|Section>]Print this usage and exit. An optional value picks the form: ascii or unicode (the default) tables, sections (the section names only), html, or a section name for that section alone.
Digest and modifications
ArgumentDescription
--enzyme <index|NoCut>The enzyme, by Carafe's index (default 1, trypsin), or NoCut for the non-specific digest, which forces 100 missed cleavages.
--miss_c <n>Maximum missed cleavages (default 2).
--minLength <n>Minimum peptide length (default 7).
--maxLength <n>Maximum peptide length (default 35).
--clip_n_mAlso digest each protein with its N-terminal methionine removed.
--fixMod <ids>Fixed modifications, Carafe's ids comma-separated (default 1, carbamidomethyl C).
--varMod <ids>Variable modifications, Carafe's ids comma-separated (default 2, oxidation M).
--maxVar <n>Maximum variable modifications per peptide (default 1).
--min_pep_mz <mz>Minimum precursor m/z (default 300).
--max_pep_mz <mz>Maximum precursor m/z (default 2000).
--min_pep_charge <z>Minimum precursor charge (default 2).
--max_pep_charge <z>Maximum precursor charge (default 3 for the FASTA build, 4 for library prediction).
--I2LConvert isoleucine to leucine in every sequence.
Entrapment FASTA build
ArgumentDescription
--entrapmentAdd entrapment peptides, shuffled from the targets, or taken from the proteins of --entrapment_db.
--no_decoysWrite no decoy peptides.
--decoy_prefix <prefix>The prefix of decoy protein names (default decoy_ for the FASTA build, rev_ for a library).
--mz_filterKeep only the peptides with a precursor m/z inside --min_pep_mz to --max_pep_mz at some charge of --min_pep_charge to --max_pep_charge, modifications counted.
--entrapment_seed <n>The seed of the entrapment shuffle (default 42).
--decoy_seed <n>The seed of the decoy generation (default 24).
--entrapment_db <proteins.fasta>A FASTA of a foreign species whose peptides are the entrapment, in place of shuffled targets.
--entrapment_ratio <ratio>Entrapment peptides per target, 0.1 to 1.0 (default 1.0).
--no_similarity_gateKeep the entrapment peptides that are near-copies of their own targets. For audit comparison only: the resulting library should not be searched.
--ignore_pairing_errorsWrite the pairing manifest even when it fails its integrity checks.
Library prediction
ArgumentDescription
--lf_type <DIA-NN|EncyclopeDIA|Skyline|blib>The library format, comma-separated for more than one: DIA-NN (default), EncyclopeDIA or Skyline, a TSV in that notation, or blib, a BiblioSpec library. Skyline with --fast writes a .blib too.
--fastWith --lf_type Skyline, write the library as a .blib; without --fast Carafe writes a generic TSV.
--lf_frag_mz_min <mz>Minimum fragment m/z (default 200).
--lf_frag_mz_max <mz>Maximum fragment m/z (default 1800).
--lf_top_n_frag <n>Fragments kept per precursor, the most intense first (default 20).
--lf_min_n_frag <n>Minimum fragments for a precursor to be written (default 2).
--lf_frag_n_min <n>Lowest fragment ordinal kept, b and y alike (default 2).
--nce <nce>The normalized collision energy the MS2 model predicts at (default 27, or a model folder's).
--ms_instrument <name>The instrument the MS2 model predicts for, by AlphaPeptDeep's name (default Eclipse, or a model folder's).
--activation <beam-CID | reCID>The activation predicted for, else a training run's. CarafeSharp only.
--analyzer <Orbitrap | LIT | ToF>The MS2 analyzer predicted for, else a training run's. CarafeSharp only.
--rt_max <minutes>The gradient length the library's retention times are scaled to, in minutes; absent, iRT.
--ccsPredict ion mobility (1/K0) for timsTOF.
--pairing_manifest <manifest.tsv>The pairing manifest of --build_entrapment_fasta: the library then carries Carafe's DecoyPairs table, every pair written whole or not at all.
--rt_reference <run>With a model trained with --rt_align kde, the training run whose minutes the library takes; else the model's, and iRT for a model of runs on different gradients. CarafeSharp only.
Models
ArgumentDescription
--model_dir <folder>A Carafe fine-tuned model folder to predict with: its MS2 and RT models, metrics and training-run summary.
--model <file.carafemodel>A saved model (.carafemodel), which every training run writes into --o: predict a library with it, or with --i fine-tune it further. CarafeSharp only.
--pretrained <pretrained_models.zip>The AlphaPeptDeep pretrained models zip (default: the one beside the executable). CarafeSharp only.
--tf <all | ms2 | rt>The models to train, or to predict with from --model_dir: all (default), ms2 or rt.
--rt_model <alphapeptdeep | chronologer>The retention-time model (default: the one of --model or --model_dir, else chronologer). A different one from a fine-tuned model's predicts with its pretrained model, Chronologer's in iRT. CarafeSharp only.
--device <cpu | gpu | cuda>Where the models run: cpu, or gpu / cuda (default; the CPU when there is no CUDA device).
--ms2_model <model>The MS2 model (a PyTorch checkpoint) fine-tuning starts from, in place of the pretrained one.
Training
ArgumentDescription
--se <Osprey>The search engine of --i: only Osprey, whose training export CarafeSharp trains on.
--fdr <q>The q-value cutoff of the precursors trained on (default 0.01).
--cor <r>The minimum fragment correlation of a precursor trained on (default 0.75).
--n_ion_min <n>The lowest b-ion ordinal trained on (default 0, every ordinal).
--c_ion_min <n>The lowest y-ion ordinal trained on (default 0, every ordinal).
--nf <n>The minimum matched fragments of a precursor trained on (default 4).
--min_n <n>The minimum valid fragments of a precursor trained on (default 4).
--validRequire a precursor's most intense fragment to be valid.
--no_maskingTrain on every ion of the kept spectra, masking none.
--seed <n>The random seed of training (default 2024; not negative).
--rt_align <none | kde>How the training runs' retention times are aligned before the RT model is fine-tuned: kde (default; each run's minutes mapped onto Chronologer's hydrophobic index by a KDE ridge on 1000 peptide forms or more, else a LOESS or a line; runs on one gradient train a model of their aligned minutes, runs on different gradients a Chronologer of the index) or none (Carafe's one rt_max). CarafeSharp only.
--rt_select <best | median>With aligned runs, the retention time a peptide form trains on: its best run, or the runs' median. CarafeSharp only.
Carafe options accepted for compatibility

Carafe 2.2.0 options these modes do not read. Each is accepted so a Carafe command line runs unchanged; one that CarafeSharp cannot honor is refused, and the rest are ignored, as Carafe ignores them.

ArgumentDescription
--itol <tolerance>Carafe's XIC extraction settings (--itol, --itolu, --sg, --min_mz, --rf, --rf_rt_win, --rt_win_offset) are Osprey's to decide: the matches, XICs and peak boundaries come from its training export, so each is reported and ignored.
--itolu <unit>See --itol.
--sg <n>See --itol.
--na <n>Flanking spectra; only 0 is supported.
--ptm_site_prob <p>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--ptm_site_qvalue <q>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--min_mz <mz>See --itol.
--lf_format <format>Carafe's library file format; parquet is not supported.
--rf_rt_win <window>See --itol.
--rt_win_offset <minutes>See --itol.
--data_type <type>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--build_koina_library <out>Not supported by CarafeSharp, which has no Koina client.
--koina_url <url>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--koina_ms2_model <name>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--koina_rt_model <name>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--nce_ms <nce>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--mode <mode>Carafe's mode; only general is supported.
--python <path>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--mod2mass <mapping>Not supported by CarafeSharp.
--user_var_mods <mods>Not supported by CarafeSharp.
--verbose <level>Accepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--ai_version <version>Carafe's Python version; only v2 is ported.
--printPTMNot supported by CarafeSharp.
--nmAccepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.
--csNot supported by CarafeSharp training.
--ezA Carafe diagnostic file (--ez, --xic, --export_mgf, --skyline) CarafeSharp does not write; reported and ignored.
--skylineSee --ez.
--use_all_peaksNot supported by CarafeSharp training.
--rfSee --itol.
--xicSee --ez.
--export_mgfSee --ez.
--y1Not supported by CarafeSharp training.
--torch_compileAccepted for Carafe compatibility; not used by CarafeSharp.

OTHER FORMS ACCEPTED: a value may follow its option (--db x.fasta) or be joined to it (--db=x.fasta), and every option is also accepted with Carafe's single dash (-db x.fasta, -db=x.fasta), so a Carafe 2.2.0 command line runs unchanged. A repeated option keeps its first value, as in Carafe.
